to spare everyone from reading the entire thread like I just did, here is the answer to natasha's question:

 "How long does it take for wastewater to get to a treatment plant once you flush?"

It takes between hours and days. I think the furthest areas of the collection system could be up to 3 or 4 days. Lowest design flow velocity is 1 ft per second so 50 miles distance would be 3 days.
Most of collection systems are gravity but there are portions that are pressurized force mains.
Usually a residential flush flows down to a low point (like down Penasquitos Canyon) then is pumped up to the north city plant on Miramar/805.  
Since Sorrento Valley pump station came up during the meeting, water from that pump station flows to the north city water recycling plant and soon Pure Water facility (indirect potable reuse system).
Most central  San Diego areas along I-8 flow down to a big pump station near the airport that pumps to Point Loma.
